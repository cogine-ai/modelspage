6. Modelspage transformations and source boundaries

Model identities are joined to the canonical catalog. Popularity combines
observed source shares using the configured 60% / 30% / 10% weights, with a
union of observed models and explicit missing values. These are Modelspage
transformations, not original source-platform rankings or an endorsement by
the sources. Field-level sources, versions and observation times remain in
the JSON's attribution, sources, profiles and model facts.

A combined score can contain components with different permissions. Applying
weights or joining identities does not expand an upstream grant. These terms
add no restrictions to MIT or CC BY material, do not limit rights provided by
law, and do not grant rights Modelspage does not hold. The applicable original
license or agreement governs its own material if this document differs from it.
The repository's software license remains separate from third-party data rights.
